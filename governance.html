Voting Rules

  • Quorum: 6 of 9 members
  • Approval: simple majority for routine; supermajority (7/9) for non-conforming or oversized commitments
  • Dissents recorded with rationale; published in audit log
  • Conflicts: members recuse for any related-party matter

Policy Alignment Checklist

  • Investment Policy Statement (IPS): commitment within asset-class band ✓
  • Concentration limits: ≤ 5% with single manager · current commitment ≤ 1% ✓
  • Vintage diversification: private markets diversified across vintages ✓
  • Currency hedging policy: per IPS, USD-base; non-USD exposure within band ✓
  • Liquidity profile: illiquid sleeve ≤ 25% of plan assets — current 18% ✓
  • ESG policy: WB Sustainable Investing Framework alignment confirmed ✓
  • Sanctions policy: zero-tolerance; OFAC/UN/EU/UK clean ✓
