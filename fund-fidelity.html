Firm Profile

Fidelity Investments (operating through FMR LLC and affiliates) is one of the largest asset managers and recordkeepers in the world. Founded in 1946 by Edward C. Johnson II in Boston, it remains privately held — controlled by the Johnson family and senior employees. Chair and CEO Abigail Johnson has led the firm since 2014.
