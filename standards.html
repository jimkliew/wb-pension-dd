Proposed Global Pension Fund DD Standard (the "World Bank Standard")

The World Bank Group is uniquely positioned to set a global standard for pension-fund manager due diligence. The principles below are derived from the agent-level controls operationalized in this platform and are proposed as a unifying framework for sovereign-, public-, and multilateral pension plans.

The Ten Pillars

  1. Multi-Agent Independence: at least seven specialist analytical functions (financial, operational, legal, ESG, risk, governance, liquidity) must operate independently with no single agent able to overrule another.
  2. Four-Eyes Principle (mandatory QA): no DD recommendation may be finalized without independent QA verification of every cited fact and re-derivation of every quantitative metric.
  3. Evidence-Based: every claim cites a primary source (regulatory filing, audit report, sponsor disclosure, or third-party data provider). Unsupported assertions are explicitly disallowed.
  4. Immutable Audit Trail: every agent action and human action is appended to a cryptographically signed log retained for not less than 25 years.
  5. Sanctions Zero-Tolerance: any match against OFAC SDN, UN, EU, or UK consolidated sanctions lists triggers immediate disqualification pending escalation.
  6. Independent Valuation Floor: ≥ 90% of NAV must be independently valued (third-party) for non-listed strategies.
  7. Liquidity Stress Floor: commitments must withstand 200% of historical-max redemption velocity without triggering involuntary asset sales.
  8. Climate Stress Disclosure: TCFD-aligned, NGFS-stressed; Scope 1+2 ≥ 80%, Scope 3 ≥ 60% portfolio coverage by 2026.
  9. Governance Documentation: board independence, key-person provisions, succession planning, and conflicts policy verifiable in writing.
  10. Continuous Monitoring: initial DD is followed by quarterly automated refresh and annual deep review for the life of the investment.

Adoption Pathway

The Standard is proposed for adoption through the following channels: (i) endorsement by IOPS and OECD WPPP at their 2027 plenary; (ii) reference adoption by the Sovereign Wealth Funds International Forum (SWFIF) and the International Forum of Sovereign Wealth Funds (IFSWF); (iii) operational adoption by World Bank Group Treasury for the WB Group Staff Retirement Plan and Trust as the reference implementation; (iv) phased rollout to client-country public pension systems via TA programs.
