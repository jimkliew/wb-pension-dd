Firm Profile

Bridgewater Associates was founded by Ray Dalio in 1975 and grew to become one of the world's largest hedge fund managers. The firm operates from Westport, Connecticut and is regulated by the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission as a Registered Investment Adviser (Form ADV: SEC# 801-43185 / CRD# 105631).

The firm pioneered the "risk parity" framework with its All Weather strategy and operates an active macro fund, Pure Alpha. Ray Dalio transitioned from CIO to mentor in October 2022; the firm operates under co-CIOs Bob Prince, Greg Jensen, and Karen Karniol-Tambour, with Nir Bar Dea as sole CEO since early 2023.

Key Public Data Points

  • Firm AUM ≈ $112.5B (down from ~$150B peak in 2019)
  • ~1,300 employees globally
  • Investor base: sovereign wealth funds, public pensions, endowments
  • Custodian: Bank of New York Mellon (per Form ADV Part 2A)
