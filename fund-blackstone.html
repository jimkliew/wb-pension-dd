Vehicle Profile

BREIT is a non-traded, perpetual-life real estate investment trust sponsored by Blackstone Inc. (NYSE: BX). Launched in January 2017, BREIT invests primarily in stabilized income-producing commercial real estate in the United States.

BREIT files Form 10-K and 10-Q with the SEC, but its shares are not listed on a public exchange. Pricing is via monthly NAV with monthly subscriptions and monthly redemptions subject to share-class lockups and program-level caps.

Liquidity Mechanics & Redemption History

Share Repurchase Plan (SRP):

  • Monthly redemptions, capped at 2% of NAV / month and 5% of NAV / quarter
  • 1-year holding-period charge (5% of NAV) for early redemptions
  • Redemptions pro-rated when oversubscribed

Activation history:

  • November 2022 – February 2024: redemption requests routinely exceeded monthly cap
  • 11 months out of 16 met cap and pro-rated
  • Cap last fully met: ~Q1 2024
  • 2024 H2: redemption pressure normalized; cap not binding since March 2024

Fees & Leverage

  • Management fee (Class I): 1.25% of NAV
  • Performance participation: 12.5% of total return over 5% hurdle, with high-water-mark
  • Front-end load (Class I): None (vs Class S = 3.5%)
  • Leverage: ~46% LTV at portfolio level (vs 50% target ceiling)
  • Average cost of debt: ~4.4% blended; ~85% fixed/hedged

Operational Infrastructure

Service Providers

  • Auditor: Deloitte & Touche LLP
  • Independent Director(s): Majority of board independent per non-listed REIT governance standards
  • Independent valuation advisor: Altus Group U.S. Inc. (third-party for ~78% of NAV)
  • Property managers: Internal Blackstone platforms + third-party operators by sector

Valuation Process

Monthly NAV produced by sponsor-affiliated advisor; ~78% of NAV cross-checked by independent appraiser annually on rolling basis. Remaining 22% relies on sponsor-internal valuation. WB threshold for independent coverage: 90%.
