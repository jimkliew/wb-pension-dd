Firm Profile

The Carlyle Group, Inc. is a publicly-traded global investment firm (NASDAQ: CG) founded in 1987 by Daniel D'Aniello, William Conway, and David Rubenstein. Headquartered in Washington, DC, with 29 offices across 5 continents.

CEO Harvey M. Schwartz joined as Chief Executive Officer in February 2023, succeeding Kewsong Lee. The firm operates across three business segments: Global Private Equity, Global Credit, and Global Investment Solutions.

Key Public Data Points (per 10-K and quarterly filings)

  • Total AUM: ~$447B (as of Q4 2024 reporting)
  • Fee-Earning AUM: ~$315B
  • Capital deployment 2024: ~$36B
  • Headcount: ~2,300 globally

Source: Carlyle Group 10-K (FY2024), filed February 2025; quarterly earnings releases.
